Welcome!

My name is Grace Fanson and I am a planetary science PhD candidate at MIT as a part of the Gaia Lab. I study Martian valley networks, leveraging their morphology (shape) to understand what the early climate was like on the Red Planet. I have a BS in Physics from Georgia Tech where I worked as a research assistant for ARES Lab and PLANETAS on potential volcanic activity in the Arabia Terra region of Mars.
